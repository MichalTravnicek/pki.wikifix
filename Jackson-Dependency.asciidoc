= Overview =

Currently PKI uses Jackson for XML/JSON mapping.

Below are Jackson dist-git packages in Fedora and the RPM packages they provide:

* link:https://src.fedoraproject.org/rpms/jackson-parent[jackson-parent]
** `jackson-parent`
* link:https://src.fedoraproject.org/rpms/jackson-bom[jackson-bom]
** `jackson-bom`
* link:https://src.fedoraproject.org/rpms/jackson-annotations[jackson-annotations]
** `jackson-annotations`
** `jackson-annotations-javadoc`
* link:https://src.fedoraproject.org/rpms/jackson-core[jackson-core]
** `jackson-core`
* link:https://src.fedoraproject.org/rpms/jackson-databind[jackson-databind]
** `jackson-databind`
* link:https://src.fedoraproject.org/rpms/jackson-jaxrs-providers[jackson-jaxrs-providers]
** `jackson-jaxrs-providers`
** `jackson-jaxrs-json-provider`
** `jackson-jaxrs-providers-datatypes`
** `jackson-jaxrs-providers-javadoc`
** `jackson-jaxrs-providers-parent`
* link:https://src.fedoraproject.org/rpms/jackson-modules-base[jackson-modules-base]
** `jackson-modules-base`
** `jackson-module-jaxb-annotations`

= Jackson Dependency for PKI 10 or Earlier =

In older Fedora Jackson library was provided by link:https://src.fedoraproject.org/rpms/jackson-modules-base[jackson-modules-base] package.
The following link:https://koji.fedoraproject.org/koji/packageinfo?packageID=23085[builds] are available:

* Fedora 32: `jackson-modules-base-2.10.x`

= Jackson Dependency for PKI 11 or Later =

In newer Fedora Jackson library is provided by link:https://src.fedoraproject.org/rpms/jackson-modules-base[jackson-modules-base] package.
The following link:https://koji.fedoraproject.org/koji/packageinfo?packageID=23085[builds] are available:

* Fedora 33-37: `jackson-modules-base-2.11.x`
* Fedora 38: `jackson-modules-base-2.14.x`
